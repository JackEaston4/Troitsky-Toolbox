# Stakeholder Interview Guide

## Description

The Fall CD interviews will be semi-structured, with questions tailored primarily to experienced members, and additionally to specific roles. Interviews will be around 10--15 minutes long, and conducted with 2--3 participants at a time, allowing for both discussion and individual responses. No names will be recorded, only information regarding the background of the members, and transcripts will be anonymized before being reported in the repository.

The interview guide is intended to be flexible allowing for experienced members to direct the discussion to areas they feel are important, while still ensuring that core concerns are addressed. As many of the interviewed members will be a part of many of these user groups, certain interviews will cover multiple question sets.

## Interview Questions

### All participants, General Questions

1. What is your role in the club, and how many years have you been involved?
2. Which tools do your teams use to design, analyze, and store their bridges?
3. What were the largest challenges you faced when you first joined the club?

### Executives

1. What information regarding the club administration is most important to consolidate?
2. When you first became an executive, what were your largest difficulties?
3. How would you like to see the software sub team integrated into the club administration?

### Team captains and experienced members

1. What tool have you found is the most accurate and useful in predicting your bridge's performance?
2. How close to a model estimate do you expect your bridge's performance to be, including uncertainties?
3. What are the most common reasons for bridge failures in your experience?
4. Does the club have models and recorded failure loads of past bridges that could be used to test the Toolbox?

### Closing, All participants

1. Is there anything about the club's information or tools that we did not ask about?
2. Are there any items that you feel are important to include in the Toolbox that we have not discussed?
