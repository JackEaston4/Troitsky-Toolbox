\subsubsection*{Jack Easton}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item What went well while writing this deliverable? 
    During writing the this deliverable, it really gave our team a great opportunity to come together and unify our perpsectives and ideas. All team members had many invaluable ideas and inputs on the project which allowed us to end up with a proposed development plan and problem statement that is greater than what we could have come up with individually.

    \item What pain points did you experience during this deliverable, and how
    did you resolve them?
    Intially when writing the content and documentation for this deliverable, it was difficult to focus all of the different ideas, options, tools, and pieces of domain knowledge provided by the Troitsky Team into a clear and articulatable form. However, through working through proven processed for development and figuring things out one question/bulletpoint at a time, I feel much more confident in my understanding and ability to convey our plan for the POC document.

    \item How did you and your team adjust the scope of your goals to ensure
    they are suitable for a Capstone project (not overly ambitious but also of
    appropriate complexity for a senior design project)?
    I think the key of the scope of our project being suitable for a Capstone project lies in the unique structure of our project. Because concept of the project is to create a toolbox of a variety of independent but specially customized tools, each tool presents isolated complexity. This naturally allows the project to be broken down into attainable sections (by tool). When viewed individually, some of the tools are highly complex (making them suitable for a senior design project), but all of the tools do not relpy on each other to present their functionality. It is additionally worth noting that a large part of the complexity of the project comes in how to correctly and efficently present a multitude of differing features while adhering to proper software architecture design principles.
\end{enumerate}
