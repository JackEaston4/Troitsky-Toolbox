\nsection{P.1 Roles and personnel}

\subsection*{P.1.1 Project Supervisor - Mason}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.1 Project Supervisor}
The project supervisor will be responsible for coordinating internal deadlines, meetings and development 
timelines. The supervisor will serve as the main point of contact with administration, the supervisor and the TA, and serve as the default 
team lead.

\subsection*{P.1.2 Troitsky Club Communication Associate - Pedro}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.2 Toritsky Club Communication Associate}
The Troitsky Club Communication Associate will be responsible for the majority of the coordination with the club 
organization and executive members. Surveys and interviews will be organized, distributed, and run by this member.

\subsection*{P.1.3 Front-end / UI Developer - Pedro, Michael}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.3 Front-end / UI Developer}
This role will be responsible for the UI of the product, both in technical displays of data and in more 
theme/design heavy components. They will build the web app's interface, so experience in web frameworks would be 
beneficial. They will be the ones primarily responsible for the usability testing in the Winter term's CD.

\subsection*{P.1.4 Back-end/Data Developer - Jack, Mason}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.4 Back-end / Data Developer}
This role will be responsible for the design of the data storage within the app. This will cover both the files and 
data the club stores (bridge files, tutorials), but also RBAC information and user/club site preferences.

\subsection*{P.1.5 Quality Assurance / Tester - Michael}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.5 Quality Assurance / Tester}
The Quality Assurance / Tester will be responsible for maintaining a standard of quality in both code and 
documentation of the web app. This will involve a review of final product content and the overall design of the 
testing within the system.

\subsection*{P.1.6 Supervisor - Isabella Vescio}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.6 Supervisor}
The supervisor for the project will provide guidance, domain expertise and general feedback for the service. As they are 
heavily involved in the club, they will also support interaction and integration with the club.

\subsection*{P.1.7 Teaching Assistant - Mohamad Mirhoseininejad}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{P.1.7 Teaching Assistant}
The TA will provide course-related feedback, answer administrative questions and provide feedback on expectations and course 
grading. They will serve as the main point of contact with course administration. General project feedback during TA meetings will also 
help keep the project timeline in check.
