\subsubsection*{McMaster Troitsky Members}
A survey was sent to Troitsky members through the club's communication channels to elicit the overall wants and needs of members.
The questions, as administered, are listed below and in the \href{https://github.com/JackEaston4/Troitsky-Toolbox/blob/main/docs/CDs/Interviews/SurveyDesign.md}{survey design record}, and the responses are available as \href{https://github.com/JackEaston4/Troitsky-Toolbox/blob/main/docs/CDs/Interviews/Troitsky%20Toolbox%20%E2%80%94%20Member%20Needs%20%26%20Service%20Priority%20Survey.csv}{raw data}.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item How many years of experience do you have in Troitsky? \textbf{[0--5]}
    \item What is your current role(s)? \textbf{[Team/General Member, Team Captain, Coach, Executive Member]}
    \item What is your modelling experience? \textbf{[None, Through some courses, Experience modelling in and out of class]}
    \item How valuable would you find an entry-level modelling service, implementing simple load testing and weak point analysis? \textbf{[1--5]}
    \item Rate (1--5) how valuable you would find having this information easily accessible, regarding past McMaster Troitsky data.
    \textbf{[Bridge modelling file, Joint construction data (specific model use), Manufacturing information, Past team results, Past technical presentation material]}
    \item How does your team store data? \textbf{[Microsoft Teams, Google Drive, Individual people hold files, Discord, Email, Other]}
    \item What has been one of the most frustrating parts of your time in the club, outside of actually building the bridge? \textbf{[Paragraph]}
    \item Rate these features on how useful you would find them. \textbf{[Would not use, Slightly useful, Useful, Very useful, Club needs this]}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
        \item Searchable archive of past bridge designs
        \item Database of material testing results (stick strength, glue, floss, etc.)
        \item Bridge weight estimator from a submitted model
        \item Structural efficiency calculation
        \item Ultimate load and failure point prediction
        \item Upload and visualise a team's bridge model in the browser
        \item Exit documents / role handover records for executive positions
        \item Onboarding guide and training material for new members
        \item Private workspace for each team's own files and data
        \item Competition rules and regulations reference
        \item Club announcements and general information page
    \end{enumerate}
    \item If we could deliver only 3 of these, which would you choose? \textbf{[A--K, up to 3]}
    \item Would you want your team files, results and progress to be available for future teams to learn from? \textbf{[Yes, No, Maybe]}
    \item If you could go back to being a new member, or if you're currently a new member, what tutorials would you want access to? \textbf{[Modelling software, Manufacturing, Slab building, Team organization, Other; select 2]}
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph*{Other Findings}
\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Knowledge Repository Data Prioritization:} When considering what data from past teams and competition years current members wish to have, 
    joint design and construction data was the most prioritized. 
    \item \textbf{Current Team Data Storage Solutions:} Most teams (13/20) utilize Microsoft Teams, with the rest spread between other common services.
    \item \textbf{Data Sharing Preference:} 17/20 teams have stated that they wish to have results, designs and work preserved and shared within the app.
    \item \textbf{Desired Tutorial Types:} For tutorials kept within the knowledge repository, there was a clear split, with more experienced members stating that 
    they wish to have manufacturing-focused tutorials, while newer, less experienced members pushed for modelling software tutorials. Results: Manufacturing 15, 
    modelling software 9, slab building 9, team organization 4; 1 person wrote in joint method selection
    \item \textbf{Major General Frustrations:} Access to past designs, reference work for guidance and orientation for new 
    members, inspiration for designs, specific joint construction and design information.
    
\end{itemize}

\paragraph*{Use of Results}

Primarily utilized during the requirement specification and modification through the SRS development (S1 & S2). The user base's 
evaluation of service value helped to determine the prioritization (S.5) for the project. User characteristics were also determined 
and expanded upon from the survey, especially considering the experience of the current members within the club.
The survey also outlines the gaps in the srs, and past misconceptions the team had about the user base.

\subsubsection*{McMaster Troitsky Executives}
Both Pedro Azevedo and our supervisor Bella Vescio are Troitsky Executives; our requirements are gathered through team meetings and supervisor meetings.
The consolidation of information and ease of transition is one of the main goals in the development of this project, so we would want to hear the executives' opinions on this.
Important Questions to Ask:
\begin{itemize}
\item What information is most important to consolidate?
\item When taking your position, what would you have liked to have seen within a transition document?
\item How would you go about outlining the duties of a software development and support team?
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{McMaster CAS Students}
McMaster CAS students are needed for the Software Development Team; 
they will maintain and expand the toolbox. The challenge of this project is the maintainability and expandability of the code, so we must have an understanding of how future software teams are set up so that we can prepare documentation accordingly. To ease the burden on them, we must elicit preferred documentation formats.
Some questions we would like to ask are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item When working with a system you did not develop yourself, rate(1-5) how helpful you find these documentation formats.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item  Markdown or other lightweight markup languages for README files.
        \item Inline Documentation (Javadoc, Doxygen, XML Doc Comments, JSDoc, Docstrings)
        \item Collaborative Wiki Formats, think online library documentation.
        \item Modelling and Visual Formats (PlantUML, C4 Model)
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
