\nsection{E.6 Invariants}
\comment{Properties of the environment that the system's operation must preserve.}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The knowledge entered into the system, including development knowledge, scientific and engineering methods,
    and veteran wisdom, shall be fully preserved.  
    \item Any model entered into the system, whether developed before or after the system's released, shall be
    fully preserved and not corrected at all to preserve development history as faithfully as possible for easy comparison.
    \item The rules of the competition that are relevant to the system shall be fully preserved and not be mutated at all.
    This is because modelling will be based of the rules as they are, and any deviation from the rules will result in inferior models.

\end{itemize}
