\nsection{E.3 Constraints}

\subsubsection*{Troitsky Toolbox Constraints}

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Competition Rules:} The model compliance checker and other support services within Troitsky Toolbox must 
comply with the rules set out for the competition. Outdated guides must be labelled to ensure users understand they do 
not fit with current competition rules.

\item \textbf{Member Data and Privacy:} As the service seeks to compile team and member information, member privacy and 
sharing permissions dictate what can be collected. During the current competition year, individual team files must be left 
separate, with only executives and coaches being able to view all files. After the competition is complete, exit documentation 
collected will determine what individuals wish to have stored for future years.

\item \textf{Club Data Format Compatibility :} The system must accommodate the file formats that the club utilizes, being 
constrained to a generalized file format when multiple are available. In the case of modelling files, STEP (.stp) files must be used 
as input as they are available across utilized services. The material testing data collection must accept .csv files, working with 
the data exporting the club utilizes.

\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Operational Constraints}

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Budget:} The funding set out by the MES, along with the yearly club sponsorships, will determine the budget and 
therefore constrain the services and upkeep of the product. Troitsky Toolbox must operate within its share of the overall club 
funds, dictating the storage capabilities and API usage of the service.

\item \textbf{Troitsky Software Subteam:} Maintenance year to year after this year will be limited by the number of software 
subteam members the club recruits. As the subteam is made up of student volunteers, the experience and drive of the members 
will also determine the level of development of the system.

\end{itemize}
