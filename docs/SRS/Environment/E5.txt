\nsection{E.5 Effects}
\comment{Elements and properties of the environment that the system will affect.}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Centralization of development information - the wealth of development information (e.g. past designs, material testing information)
     will be in one central location for future members to access, which will facilitate onboarding.
    \item Model analysis - team members will be able to submit their models and recieve key metrics and predictions regarding
    that model through an automatic analysis.
    \item Facilitated comparison - team members will be able to easily compare modelling results for different models. This will
    facilitate development because team members will be able to start from the best and abandon changes that caused the model to degrade.
    \item Simplified knowledge transfer - veteran team members will be able to share their vast knowledge with new members more easily.
    This is critical, especially for outgoing team members.
    \item Creation of a permanant software sub-team - this project will be the genesis of a permanant sofware subteam of the club.
    Furthermore, brief but top-tier resources alongside the software suitewill be developed to smooth the onboarding 
    of new software subteam members.
\end{itemize}
