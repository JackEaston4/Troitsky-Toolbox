\centering

\textbf{Change} & \textbf{Reason} \\
\midrule
Added the Appendix book (A) after the Project book. & The Meyer template has only the Goals, Environment, System and Project books. The course rubric and SRS checklist require content that belongs in none of them (A.1, A.3, A.4). \\
Added a References section after the Project book. & The Meyer template has no references section. It lists the sources cited in the document, such as those for the values in A.1. \\
Added A.1 Symbolic Constants. & The SRS checklist requires the values of symbolic constants to be given in a table in the Appendix. \\
Added A.2 Changes from Template. & The SRS checklist requires changes made to the template to be described in the document. \\
Added A.3 Generative AI Use Disclosure. & Required by the course rubric. \\
Added A.4 Reflection. & Required by the course rubric, using the questions from the course's SRS reflection template. \\
