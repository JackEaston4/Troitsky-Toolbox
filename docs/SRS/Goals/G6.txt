\nsection{G.6 Limitations and exclusions}
Below is a list of limitations for the system, items that are currently out of scope for development:

\begin{itemize}
    \item The system is not looking to replace the more complex modelling capabilities of external/industry standard
    modelling software. Unlike the current apps being used by teams, such as Autodesk Inventor, SAP2000 and SolidWorks, the 
    modelling component within Troitsky Toolbox will be intentionally simplified, which will leave many of the more advanced 
    features out of scope.

    \item The system will not support user access for past members. The service will only be accessible by current members, with only 
    the public-facing landing page and gallery available to others.

    \item The system will not replace all forms of communication between members. Given the accepted use of other platforms for communication 
    and the ease of use in utilizing an existing app for communication, peer-to-peer or individual team channels will not be supported in the 
    toolbox.

    \item The system will not replace registration into the club; initial applications into specific roles will remain external. 
    Payment support will be excluded and done externally from the service with registration.

    
    
\end{itemize}
