\subsection*{G.7.1 - Direct Stakeholders}
\textbf{McMaster Troitsky Executives}\\ 
This toolbox web service was requested by the Troitsky Executives; 
they have noticed issues with information carryover during executive transitions,
as well as information and technical skill consolidation within the most senior teams. 
They would like a centralized application that can address these issues.
\\\\
\underline{Persona: Mohammed Lee}\\
Mohammed is a VP on the Troitsky Exec team. He has competed in Troitsky three times: twice as a team member and once as a coach.
Through his experience on the team, he has developed a wealth of knowledge in both the administrative and technical sides of the club. 
He is graduating next year and is worried about the transition process for the new VP taking his place.
\\\\
\textbf{General Troitsky Members}\\
General members will be the main users and the primary focus of our project. These users will both support and expand the data the
system provides, and will be the main beneficiaries of the system.
\\\\
\underline{Persona: Tenun Grigoryan}\\
Tenun is a second-year member of Troitsky. He has competed on a team once before and is hoping to compete again this year.
In his first year, there was a steep learning curve in the CAD design aspect of the competition, and he had to rely heavily on senior Troitsky members for tutorials and help. 
He hopes that this year, more resources will be provided to junior club members to decrease the barriers to competing.
\\\\
\textbf{Future Troitsky Members}\\
Future Troitsky members are students coming to McMaster who will carry the torch of the Troitsky Club.
This toolbox will lower the barrier to entry for new teams wanting to join Troitsky 
through both access to previous teams’ work and documentation, as well as scripted load testing and weak-point analysis.
\\\\
\underline{Persona: Sui Smith}\\
Sui is a high school senior who has just been accepted into the McMaster Civil Engineering Program. She is part of student parliament at her school and competes in robotics competitions as one of her extracurriculars. 
She would love to continue competing on a technical team that aligns with her program. It goes without saying that she would prioritize joining a club that provides good resources for its introductory members.
\\\\
\textbf{McMaster CAS Students}\\
Troitsky will need a software development team to maintain and extend the toolbox. 
This team will provide new extracurricular activities for CAS students at McMaster, 
allowing them to develop essential software engineering skills that will give them a leg up in school and work.
\\\\
\underline{Persona: Kyle Levi}\\
Kyle is a third-year CS student. He wants to work in large-scale system design and management when he graduates; 
he would like to join a technical team that focuses on maintaining and expanding a software system.
\\\\
\textbf{MES - McMaster Engineering Society}\\
The MES organizes engineering clubs and seeks to benefit from the legitimization of the McMaster Troitsky Club through a centralized information platform. 
The MES also provides funding for the McMaster Troitsky Club, so the hosting of services and associated costs may be dependent on
the given budget.
\\\\
\underline{Persona: Mia Tanaka}\\
Mia is the treasurer for the MES. She would like to ensure that the club funding she is approving is going to clubs that are investing in their long-term sustainability and development.

\subsection*{G.7.2 - Indirect Stakeholders}
\textbf{Concordia University Troitsky Organization}\\
As Concordia University hosts the Troitsky competition, it establishes
the rules and regulations of the competition and therefore influences what
is prioritized by the direct stakeholders. This would influence the design
of the system and which features would benefit teams the most.
\\\\
\textbf{McMaster University}\\
The university itself is an indirect stakeholder, as it benefits from the success of the McMaster Troitsky Club and the success of teams during the
Troitsky competition through an improved reputation as a school that produces capable students.
