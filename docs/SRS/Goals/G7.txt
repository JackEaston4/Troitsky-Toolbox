\subsection*{G.7.1 - Direct Stakeholders}

\textbf{McMaster Troitsky Executives}\\ 
This toolbox webservice was requested by Troitsky Executives; 
they have been noticing issues will information carryover during exeecutive transitions,
as well as information and technical skill consolidation in the most senior teams. 
They would like a centralized applciation that can address these issues.

\textbf{Returning Troitsky Members}
Returning Members often have thier previous years work stored over multiple services and devices.
Additionally, they may have had help and direction from senior teams the previous years who are no longer here. 
To wasily preform at the level they did in previous years ease of access the thier previous work and others previous work is essential.
