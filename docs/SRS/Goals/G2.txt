\nsection{G.2 Current situation}

Currently, teams operate in a highly individualistic manner, with the greater McMaster Troitsky club covering the administrative details regarding competition registration and providing 
guidance and feedback when possible. Teams store information through Microsoft Teams, Google Drive and emails between members, with many teams resulting in having a single member in charge 
of different competition components. Currently, the club as a whole does not have a centralized system in place to store past designs, bridge models or technical details, only having more adiminstrative/executive 
documents stored. Even tutorials that are consistently useful for teams do not have a consistent access point and therefore become unreliable as proper support for the club. 
Despite these challenges, for the past 2 years the club has been pushing for more integration between all the teams, generalizing the coach positions and creating a stronger 
administrative presence through timelines and events.
